# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA/Test Engineers help the team deliver reliable software by defining how quality is validated throughout development and before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and maintain appropriate regression coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and report defects with clear reproduction details
- Identify quality risks and contribute to release-readiness decisions
- Promote testability and effective test automation

### Goals
- Detect defects early and reduce regressions
- Provide clear evidence of product quality and release risks
- Ensure delivered behavior meets agreed acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Quality and release-readiness updates
- Testability discussions during refinement and code review

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Agree on testability, automate tests, and triage defects together.
- **Product Managers:** Clarify acceptance criteria and communicate quality implications of scope or behavior.
- **Project Managers:** Report quality risks, coverage, and readiness against release checkpoints.

---

## Engineering Managers / Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Tech Leads provide technical direction and support the engineering team in making sustainable delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical trade-offs
- Set expectations for code quality and engineering practices
- Advise on team capacity, sequencing, and technical dependencies
- Mentor developers and help resolve technical blockers

### Goals
- Enable maintainable, secure, and reliable solutions
- Align technical decisions with product outcomes and delivery constraints
- Help the team deliver predictably while building engineering capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs, architecture reviews, and code reviews
- Capacity and dependency discussions during planning
- Engineering updates and mentoring conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide implementation guidance, review technical choices, and remove engineering blockers.
- **Product Managers:** Assess feasibility and trade-offs, and advise on technical scope needed to achieve outcomes.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate sequencing, capacity assumptions, and technical dependency mitigation.

---

## UX/UI Designers or Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers help teams understand user needs and shape accessible, usable experiences that address them.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and define user flows and interaction patterns
- Create and validate designs, prototypes, and usability improvements
- Provide accessibility guidance and document relevant design decisions
- Share research findings and usability risks with the team

### Goals
- Make product experiences useful, clear, and accessible
- Validate design decisions with evidence from users
- Reduce usability issues before and after release

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and usability reports
- Design files, prototypes, and design reviews
- Accessibility and interaction guidance during refinement

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Explain design intent and accessibility requirements, and resolve implementation questions collaboratively.
- **Product Managers:** Frame user problems, validate outcomes, and prioritize design work using research findings.
- **Project Managers:** Identify research and design dependencies, timing, and usability risks for delivery plans.

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers provide the automation and operational foundations needed to build, deploy, and run software reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, development environments, and deployment automation
- Improve service reliability, observability, and operational readiness
- Define rollback and recovery practices for releases
- Identify platform constraints and operational risks

### Goals
- Make delivery repeatable and reduce deployment risk
- Keep environments and services reliable and observable
- Help teams respond effectively to operational issues

### Typical Communication
- Pipeline, environment, and deployment documentation
- Operational readiness and incident updates
- Release planning and reliability reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborate on build and deployment automation, environments, and service observability.
- **Product Managers:** Explain platform capabilities and operational trade-offs that affect product scope or availability.
- **Project Managers:** Communicate environment dependencies, release constraints, and operational risks for planning.

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance requirements throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define applicable security and compliance requirements and controls
- Review threats, risks, and relevant design or implementation choices
- Advise on secure development and evidence needed for compliance
- Clarify incident escalation expectations and support security response

### Goals
- Protect users, systems, and sensitive information
- Meet applicable compliance obligations without avoidable delivery surprises
- Make security risks and required mitigations visible to decision-makers

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements, threat reviews, and risk assessments
- Compliance evidence and control reviews
- Security advisories and incident escalation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Advise on secure implementation, review relevant risks, and agree on mitigations.
- **Product Managers:** Clarify security and compliance requirements that affect product scope and user outcomes.
- **Project Managers:** Surface security risks, required review checkpoints, and escalation paths in delivery plans.

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success Representatives bring customer feedback and service experience into product delivery and release planning.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer issues, needs, and usage trends
- Review support readiness and customer-facing release communications
- Prepare support guidance and identify training needs for changes
- Share customer impact and emerging concerns with the delivery team

### Goals
- Help customers adopt changes and resolve issues effectively
- Ensure releases are supported with accurate, timely information
- Make customer impact visible in product and delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and support trend reports
- Release notes, help content, and support enablement materials
- Escalations about customer-impacting issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Share actionable issue reports and recurring pain points to support investigation and prioritization.
- **Product Managers:** Provide customer evidence to inform priorities, product decisions, and success measures.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate support readiness, release communications, and escalation of customer-impacting risks.
